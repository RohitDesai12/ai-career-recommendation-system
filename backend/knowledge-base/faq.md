# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions
- **Q: What is Career Compass?**
  - A: Career Compass is an AI-driven career acceleration platform that helps students discover career paths, learn via customized roadmaps, build ATS-optimized resumes, prepare for interviews, and apply directly to verified employer job postings.
- **Q: How do I create a new resume?**
  - A: Go to Resume Studio (`/resume-studio`) and click "Create Resume" or navigate to `/resume-studio/create`. You can start from your profile, upload an existing resume, or build from scratch.
- **Q: Where can I see my submitted applications?**
  - A: Go to Applications & Jobs in the sidebar and select "Applications" or navigate to `/jobs?tab=applications`.
- **Q: Where are my saved jobs?**
  - A: Open the sidebar under Applications & Jobs and click "Saved Jobs" or navigate to `/jobs?tab=saved`.
- **Q: How does the Career Test work?**
  - A: Go to `/assessment` to take the multi-dimensional assessment. The system analyzes your technical competencies, personality, and interests to recommend optimal roles with match percentages.
- **Q: How do I start a Mock Interview?**
  - A: Navigate to `/mock-interview` from the sidebar under Practice & Coaching. You can choose a technical track and practice speaking or typing your answers.
- **Q: Can Career Compass automatically apply to jobs for me without my consent?**
  - A: No, Career Compass allows 1-Click manual application submission where you review the role and choose which resume to submit.
- **Q: Is my data private?**
  - A: Yes, your profile, resumes, test scores, and applications are private to your account and only shared with employers when you choose to submit an application.
- **Q: Can Career Compass book in-person interviews or flights for me?**
  - A: No, Career Compass connects candidates with recruiters and tracks interview invitations on the platform, but does not book travel or external calendar bookings directly.
