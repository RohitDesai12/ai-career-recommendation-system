# Resume Studio Documentation

Resume Studio (`/resume-studio`) is an all-in-one suite for creating, optimizing, analyzing, tailoring, and managing professional resumes.

## How to Access Resume Studio
- Navigate to **Resume Studio** in the sidebar navigation or go directly to `/resume-studio`.
- From the overview page, you can see your active resumes, average ATS score, quick actions, and recent edits.

## Features & Workflows
1. **Create Hub (`/resume-studio/create`)**:
   - **Start from Profile**: Automatically imports your saved education, skills, projects, and work experience from your Career Compass profile.
   - **Import Existing Resume**: Upload an existing PDF or DOCX file to extract and populate resume sections automatically.
   - **Start from Scratch**: Begin with a clean template and fill in your details step-by-step.

2. **Resume Builder (`/resume-studio/builder`)**:
   - Interactive visual resume builder with sections for Personal Info, Summary, Work Experience, Education, Skills, Projects, Certifications, Achievements, and Languages.
   - Live PDF preview and real-time formatting controls (font family, font size, accent color, spacing).
   - Instant export to clean PDF.

3. **Templates Gallery (`/resume-studio/templates`)**:
   - Choose from multiple ATS-compliant templates: Modern, Minimalist, Executive, Tech Specialist, and Creative.
   - Switch templates anytime with a single click without losing entered content.

4. **Tailor for Job (`/resume-studio/tailor`)**:
   - Select one of your saved resumes and paste the target job description.
   - AI highlights missing keywords, suggests optimized bullet points with strong action verbs, and aligns skills with the employer requirements.

5. **My Resumes (`/resume-studio/my-resumes`)**:
   - View, duplicate, edit, download, or delete your saved resumes.
   - Filter by target role and view last modified dates.

6. **Portfolio Builder (`/resume-studio/portfolio`)**:
   - Automatically generates a modern web portfolio showcasing your projects, skills, and resume details.
