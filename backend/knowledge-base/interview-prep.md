# Interview Preparation & Mock Interview Documentation

Career Compass provides two comprehensive tools to prepare candidates for high-stakes technical, system design, and behavioral interviews.

## Interview Prep Guide (`/interview-prep`)
- **Categorized Question Banks**:
  - Data Structures & Algorithms (Arrays, Linked Lists, Trees, Graphs, Dynamic Programming)
  - System Design & Scalability (Databases, Caching, Load Balancing, Microservices)
  - Core Engineering Concepts (OOP, Operating Systems, Networking, DBMS)
  - Behavioral & Leadership (STAR method templates, leadership principles, situational questions)
- **Solved Tracker**: Track how many problems and topics you have solved.
- **Bookmarks (`/saved-preps`)**: Save challenging questions and personalized notes for quick revision.

## Mock Interview Simulator (`/mock-interview`)
- **Interactive Simulation**: Practice with an AI interviewer via voice or interactive text.
- **Dynamic Difficulty**: Technical follow-up questions adapt to your responses in real time.
- **Comprehensive Scorecard**:
  - Technical accuracy score
  - Communication clarity score
  - Problem-solving adaptability score
  - Strengths and missed points summary
  - Overall hiring verdict (Strong Hire, Hire, Leaning Hire, Needs Improvement)
