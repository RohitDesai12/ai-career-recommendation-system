# Cover Letter Generator Documentation

Cover Letter Generator (`/resume-studio/cover-letter`) allows students to generate tailored, highly persuasive cover letters for specific job applications.

## How to Generate a Cover Letter
1. Navigate to **Resume Studio -> Cover Letter** or go to `/resume-studio/cover-letter`.
2. Select your base resume or allow the generator to use your Career Compass profile.
3. Enter the **Company Name**, **Job Title / Role**, and paste the **Job Description** (or select a saved job).
4. Choose your preferred **Tone**:
   - *Professional & Confident* (default for enterprise and corporate roles)
   - *Passionate & Enthusiastic* (ideal for startups and high-growth teams)
   - *Technical & Direct* (focuses heavily on engineering accomplishments and architectures)
5. Click **Generate Cover Letter**.
6. Review, edit inline, copy to clipboard, or export as PDF.
