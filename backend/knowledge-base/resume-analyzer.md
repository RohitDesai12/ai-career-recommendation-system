# Resume Analyzer Documentation

Resume Analyzer (`/resume`) provides instant AI-powered Applicant Tracking System (ATS) scoring and recommendations for your resume.

## How It Works
1. Navigate to **Resume Studio -> Resume Analyzer** or `/resume`.
2. Select your engineering branch (CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, Data Science, etc.) and your targeted career role (e.g., Full Stack Developer, ML Engineer, DevOps Engineer).
3. Upload your resume in PDF format (or select an existing resume created in Resume Studio).
4. Click **Analyze Resume**.

## Metrics & Feedback Generated
- **Overall ATS Score (0–100%)**: Measures overall formatting, keyword density, section structure, and readability.
- **Shortlist Probability**: Predicts likelihood of passing initial automated recruiter screenings.
- **Keyword & Skill Synergy**: Compares skills present on your resume against industry benchmarks for your chosen role.
- **Top Strengths**: Highlights strong bullet points, quantified impact metrics, and relevant technical competencies.
- **Improvement Areas**: Pinpoints missing core technical keywords, weak action verbs, formatting issues, or missing sections.
- **Section-by-Section Breakdown**: Contact info, summary, experience, education, projects, and skills.
