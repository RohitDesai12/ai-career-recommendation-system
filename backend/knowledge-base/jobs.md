# Job Search, Saved Jobs & Applications Documentation

Career Compass provides an integrated job application pipeline connecting students directly with verified employer job openings.

## Exploring & Applying for Jobs (`/jobs`)
- **Search & Filter**: Search by job title, company name, location, or required skills.
- **Match Score**: View how well your profile and skills align with each opening (e.g. 92% Match).
- **1-Click Apply**: Submit your profile and selected resume directly to the employer.
- **Job Details**: View salary ranges, employment type (Full-Time, Internship, Contract), work mode (Remote, Hybrid, On-site), required qualifications, and company overview.

## Saved Jobs & Bookmarks (`/jobs?tab=saved`)
- Click the bookmark icon on any job card to save it to your personal watchlist.
- View and manage your saved jobs under **Applications & Jobs -> Saved Jobs** or `/jobs?tab=saved`.
- Sort saved jobs by recently added, match score, or salary.

## Live Application Tracker (`/jobs?tab=applications`)
- Track the live status of all submitted job applications:
  - **Applied**: Application submitted to recruiter.
  - **Reviewed**: Recruiter viewed your profile.
  - **Shortlisted**: Moved to the candidate shortlist.
  - **Interview**: Interview scheduled or invitation sent.
  - **Hired**: Offer extended or candidate hired.
  - **Rejected / Withdrawn**: Application closed.
