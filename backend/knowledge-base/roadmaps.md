# Career Roadmaps Documentation

Career Roadmaps (`/roadmap`) provides stage-by-stage structured learning paths tailored to your target career path.

## How to Create and Follow a Roadmap
1. Complete the Career Test (`/assessment`) or select a career directly from Roadmaps (`/roadmap/roadmaps`).
2. Choose from curated domains: Software Engineering, Data Science, AI/ML, Cloud & DevOps, Cybersecurity, Full Stack Web Development, Embedded Systems, and more.
3. Click **Generate Roadmap** to create a custom milestone pathway.

## Roadmap Structure
- **Stages & Milestones**: Each roadmap is broken into sequential stages (e.g. Stage 1: Foundations, Stage 2: Core Technologies, Stage 3: Advanced Architecture & Projects, Stage 4: Interview & Placement).
- **Skill Checklist**: Check off skills as you learn and master them to track your overall readiness percentage.
- **Curated Documentation & Free Courses**: Direct links to documentation, tutorials, and free resources.
- **Capstone Projects**: Recommended real-world projects with starter specifications and GitHub repository guidelines.
- **Tracking**: View your progress in the Overview tab (`/roadmap/overview`) and review archived roadmaps in History (`/roadmap/history`).
