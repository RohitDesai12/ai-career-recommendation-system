# Career Compass Platform Overview

Career Compass is an end-to-end, AI-powered career intelligence and readiness platform designed for students, job seekers, and recruiters.

## Key Student Modules & Features
1. **Dashboard (`/dashboard`)**: Central career hub displaying real-time roadmap milestones, application pipeline counts, ATS readiness score, recommended courses, and quick actions.
2. **Career Test (`/assessment`)**: Comprehensive multi-dimensional assessment assessing technical skills, personality, problem-solving, and interests to generate AI-predicted career matches.
3. **Career Results (`/results`)**: Detailed breakdown of career match percentages, market trends, salary ranges, and recommended pathways.
4. **Roadmaps (`/roadmap`)**: Interactive, stage-by-stage learning pathways with milestones, skill checklist, curated documentation, courses, and project assignments.
5. **Resume Studio (`/resume-studio`)**: Complete resume creation & optimization suite including:
   - **Create Hub (`/resume-studio/create`)**: Start from profile, existing resume, or blank canvas.
   - **Templates Gallery (`/resume-studio/templates`)**: Industry-standard, ATS-friendly resume templates.
   - **Resume Builder (`/resume-studio/builder`)**: Rich drag-and-drop / structured resume editor with instant PDF preview and export.
   - **Resume Analyzer (`/resume`)**: Real-time ATS parser analyzing match percentage, key strengths, missing keywords, and readability.
   - **Tailor for Job (`/resume-studio/tailor`)**: Tailor resume bullets specifically to a targeted job description.
   - **Cover Letter Generator (`/resume-studio/cover-letter`)**: AI-generated tailored cover letters matching job descriptions.
   - **My Resumes (`/resume-studio/my-resumes`)**: Manage all saved resumes.
   - **Score History (`/resume-studio/score-history`)**: Track resume ATS scores over time.
   - **Portfolio Builder (`/resume-studio/portfolio`)**: Generate public developer portfolios.
6. **Curated Courses (`/courses`)**: Catalog of free and verified learning courses mapped to skills.
7. **Job Search & Applications (`/jobs`)**:
   - **Explore Jobs (`/jobs`)**: Search verified openings, view match scores, filter by location, work mode, and experience.
   - **Applications (`/jobs?tab=applications`)**: Live tracking funnel (Applied, Reviewed, Shortlisted, Interview, Hired, Rejected).
   - **Saved Jobs (`/jobs?tab=saved`)**: Bookmarked positions to apply for later.
8. **Interview Prep (`/interview-prep`)**: Question banks across Data Structures, Algorithms, System Design, and Behavioral HR rounds with solved tracker.
9. **Mock Interview (`/mock-interview`)**: Voice and text interactive mock interview simulator providing instant feedback, scoring rubrics, and hiring verdicts.
10. **Profile (`/profile`)**: Manage personal details, academic background, technical skills, projects, certifications, and career preferences.
11. **Career Preferences (`/career-preferences`)**: Set preferred roles, minimum salary, work modes (Remote, Hybrid, On-site), and job alert frequencies.
12. **Notifications (`/notifications`)**: Live alerts on job postings, application status updates, and milestone achievements.
